# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss evidence and technical disagreements without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or unwanted contact. Do not disclose another person's private information, credentials, or private code.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may remove harmful content or limit participation to protect contributors. Apply moderation consistently and give affected people a clear explanation where doing so does not expose private information or create further harm.
